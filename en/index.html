Questions & answers

Do I need an international licence?

Yes. For the rental agreement you need your passport, an International Driving Permit (IDP) and your national driving licence.

How much is the deposit?

$200 for trips within Khanh Hoa province and $400 for all of Vietnam. It is refunded if the car has no damage and there are no fines; otherwise the matching amount is withheld.

Can I drive to Da Lat or further?

Yes, with the $400 deposit. The limit is 250 km per day, added up over the whole rental: 3 days = 750 km. Each km over the limit is 5,000 ₫.

Where do I pick up the car?

We agree on the hand-over spot when you book: your hotel, an address in Nha Trang or Cam Ranh airport.

How do I book?

Pick a car and dates in the form above and press “Send request” — we’ll message you. Or write to us directly on WhatsApp or Telegram.

What are the pick-up and return times?

For 1 day — pick up from 7:00, return by 22:00 the same day. For 2 days or more — return by 22:00 on the last day.

How much does it cost to rent a car in Nha Trang?

From 700,000 ₫ a day for the VinFast VF3 to 2,100,000 ₫ for the Kia Carnival. Sedans start at 900,000 ₫ and 7-seaters at 1,000,000 ₫. Prices are shown on each car above.

Is a monthly rental cheaper?

Yes. For a month or longer the daily price is noticeably lower — send us your dates and the car via the form or WhatsApp/Telegram and we will quote the rate.

Where can I rent a car in Nha Trang?

Right here: pick a car and dates in the form above — you rent directly from the owner, no middlemen. We hand over the car at your hotel, an address in Nha Trang or Cam Ranh airport.

Can I rent a car without a deposit?

A deposit is required: $200 for trips within Khanh Hoa province and $400 across Vietnam. It is refunded if the car has no damage and there are no fines.
